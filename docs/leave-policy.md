# Leave and Time Off Policy

## Types of Leave

### Annual Leave
- Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year
- Leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance
- Maximum 10 consecutive days unless approved by department head

### Sick Leave
- 10 days of paid sick leave per year
- Medical certificate required for absences over 3 consecutive days
- Can be taken without advance notice

### Emergency Leave
- Up to 3 days per year for family emergencies
- Must notify manager as soon as possible

## How to Request Leave

1. Log in to the HR Portal at https://hr.internal.com
2. Go to "My Leave" → "Request New Leave"
3. Select leave type (Annual / Sick / Emergency)
4. Enter start date and end date
5. Add a reason (optional for annual leave, required for emergency)
6. Click "Submit Request"

Your manager will receive an automatic notification and must approve within 2 business days.

## Leave Balance

You can view your remaining leave balance at https://hr.internal.com/leave/balance

## Approval Process

Leave requests are automatically routed to your direct manager.
If your manager is unavailable, requests escalate to the department head.
