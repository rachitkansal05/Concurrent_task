# Company Expense Policy

## Overview

All employees are eligible to claim expenses for legitimate business activities.
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
The expense submission deadline is the 5th of each month for the previous month's expenses.

## Eligible Expenses

The following categories of expenses are reimbursable:
- Travel (flights, hotels, ground transportation) for business trips
- Meals during client meetings or business travel (up to $75 per day)
- Software and tools required for work (requires manager pre-approval over $100)
- Conference registration fees (requires manager pre-approval)
- Office supplies (up to $50 without pre-approval)

## How to Submit an Expense Report

1. Log in to the Expense Portal at https://expenses.internal.com
2. Click "New Report" in the top navigation
3. Enter a descriptive report name (e.g., "Q3 2026 Travel — NYC Conference")
4. Add individual expense line items with receipts
5. Select the appropriate cost center from the dropdown
6. Click "Submit for Approval"
7. Your manager will receive an automatic approval request by email

## Approval Workflow

- Expenses under $500: Approved by direct manager only
- Expenses $500–$2,000: Requires manager + Finance Manager approval
- Expenses over $2,000: Requires manager + Finance Manager + CFO approval

## Reimbursement Timeline

Once fully approved, reimbursement is processed in the next payroll cycle.
Payroll runs on the 15th and last day of each month.

## Receipts

Receipts are required for all expenses over $25.
Accepted formats: PDF, PNG, JPG.
Upload receipts directly in the Expense Portal when creating the report.
